# Investment Philosophy

CatalystAI searches for the intersection of:

- an exceptional long-duration business,
- a temporary gap between price and fundamental value,
- at least one credible path for market expectations to change, and
- favorable upside relative to permanent-loss risk.

The framework deliberately separates business quality from entry timing. A great company is not automatically a great investment at every price, and a cheap security is not automatically mispriced.

## Decision layers

1. **Compounder:** Does the business deserve long-term ownership?
2. **Opportunity:** Is current pessimism or dislocation likely temporary?
3. **Catalyst:** What could change market expectations, and on what horizon?
4. **Expectations:** How much success or failure is already embedded in price?
5. **Asymmetry:** What are probability-weighted upside and permanent downside?
6. **Context:** Do macro, industry cycle, and institutional evidence support or weaken the thesis?
7. **Execution:** Does VWAPPlus PRO provide a disciplined entry and invalidation?

Every conclusion should distinguish observed facts, estimates, and judgment.
