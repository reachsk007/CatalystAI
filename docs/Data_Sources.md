# Data Sources

All inputs must carry source, effective date, publication date, ingestion date, and revision metadata where applicable.

Candidate source categories:

- SEC filings and structured financial statements
- Form 4 insider transactions
- 13F institutional holdings
- market prices and corporate actions
- earnings estimates and revisions
- options, short interest, and positioning data
- macroeconomic and credit series
- company disclosures and catalyst calendars

Vendor licensing, redistribution rights, coverage, latency, revisions, and point-in-time availability must be reviewed before a source becomes a production dependency.
