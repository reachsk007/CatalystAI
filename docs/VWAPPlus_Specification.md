# VWAPPlus PRO Specification

VWAPPlus PRO is the execution layer, not the fundamental research engine.

Planned capabilities include:

- session, weekly, monthly, and anchored VWAP,
- market and sector relative strength,
- price-volume regime classification,
- accumulation and distribution evidence,
- liquidity sweeps and failed breakdowns,
- crash capitulation and reclaim patterns,
- entry zones, invalidation, targets, and position sizing,
- manual CatalystAI score inputs, and
- non-repainting alerts and testable strategy variants.

The existing VWAPPlus master script should be imported and baselined before implementation begins.
